## From a star map to an illustration

Choose a single constellation, the twelve zodiac signs or the whole sky. Stars use real positions, brightness and colours. Keep that natural look, or change the shapes, colours and lines to suit your composition.

Eight presets give you a starting point. Each one can be adjusted.

## Draw it on

Animate **Lines Progress** from 0 to 100%. Lines draw across the constellation, and stars appear as the lines reach them. Change the build order, overlap and easing, or animate stars separately with **Stars Progress**.

| 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| ![Build at 25 percent](assets/build-25.png) | ![Build at 50 percent](assets/build-50.png) | ![Build at 75 percent](assets/build-75.png) | ![Build at 100 percent](assets/build-100.png) |

For continuous motion, add twinkle, pulse, float, wobble or sky drift. Lines support taper, animated dashes and trim paths.

## Build the sky around it

Add a Milky Way band, nebula clouds, a spiral galaxy or aurora curtains. Each has its own controls and built-in motion.

| Milky Way | Nebula |
| :---: | :---: |
| ![Milky Way band](assets/milky-way.png) | ![Nebula clouds](assets/nebula.png) |
| **Spiral galaxy** | **Aurora** |
| ![Spiral galaxy](assets/spiral-galaxy.png) | ![Teal and violet aurora](assets/aurora.png) |

The effect can follow the composition camera. Set different depths for the constellation, background stars, galaxy and aurora to get parallax as the camera moves. Keep the effect on a **2D solid**; it reads the camera itself.

## Quick start

1. [Buy Constellations](https://sensemv.gumroad.com/l/constellations), close After Effects and run the installer from your download. Select your After Effects version, finish installation and restart After Effects.
2. Create a solid the size of your composition.
3. Apply **Effect → Sky → Constellations**. Orion appears on a transparent background.
4. Pick a **Look Preset**, then choose your constellation.
5. Keyframe **Lines Progress** from **0%** to **100%**.

Pick the preset before fine-tuning the style: changing presets replaces the star, line, glow and related style settings. Your constellation, view, progress keyframes, galaxy, aurora and camera settings stay in place.

| --- | --- |
| **After Effects** | 2024 or newer; tested on 24.5 and 26.3 |
| **Windows** | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

| **Colour depth** | 8, 16 and 32 bits per channel |
| **Rendering** | CPU rendering, Multi-Frame Rendering; no GPU required |
| **Output** | Transparent, original layer or solid colour |

For faster previews, use Half or Quarter resolution. Galaxy and aurora are the heaviest parts of the effect.
